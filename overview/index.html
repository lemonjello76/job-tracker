PROJECT OVERVIEW

JOB TRACKER

A field-documentation tool that captures every drive, workday, photo, receipt, and conversation on every MistAmerica job — and turns it into a clean, organized closeout package automatically.

1

Cloud sync indicator

The cloud icon pulses when data is moving between my phone, my laptop, and the secure cloud backup. Every photo and segment is backed up the moment it's captured — even at job sites with spotty service, it queues and syncs the second I'm back online.

2

Trip grouping

Multi-job road trips (e.g. four CFA installs across Florida) get grouped under one "trip" so reports roll up by job AND by trip. Solo jobs work the same way without the grouping.

3

📧 End-of-day email button

One tap compiles today's activity — time on site, drive time, miles, photos, expenses, and self-notes — into an email pre-addressed to the office. After 4pm the app prompts me automatically.

4

Job info

Client, location, dates, and crew size — the basics for every job. The "industry-standard crew" field lets the report flag jobs that should have had more hands.

5

Geofence pin

Tapping "Pin Spot" on arrival drops a GPS anchor at the job site. From then on, the app auto-tags any time I'm within 1/2 mile as "on site" and outside that as "drive" — no manual switching needed.

6

Live dashboard

Drive hours, on-site hours, miles, and expenses update live as the day unfolds. No spreadsheet tally at the end — the numbers are always current.

7

GPS-stamped time tracking

"Start Drive" and "Start Workday" buttons begin GPS-tracked time segments. Each segment captures start time, end time, GPS coordinates, and (for drives) odometer photos AI-read for mileage.

8

Quick gas log

Single-tap shortcut for the most common receipt type. Snap the gas receipt → AI extracts the amount, vendor, and category automatically.

9

Time log

A running list of every GPS-stamped segment for this job — drive in, workday on Tuesday, drive home — with start/end times, locations, and durations. The legal-grade record behind every hours total.

10

Feature grid

One-tap captures for everything that happens on a job: Photos (auto-described by AI, GPS-stamped, grouped in reports by system — Equipment / Fans / Switching / Site — with a 🚩 flag any photo as a "discrepancy" + proposed solution), Videos (POV clips from Insta360 GO 3 or phone — auto-thumbnailed, click any thumbnail in the report to play in-place), Receipts (AI extracts vendor/amount/category), Job Files (PDFs, work orders, spec drawings), Notes, Contacts (everyone I spoke to + role), Checklist (job-type-specific templates with FINAL TOUCHES section), Comm Log (every email/text/call sent + whether they responded), Discrepancies (spec vs. actual issues flagged in a running log), and a fallback Manual Hours entry.

11

Daily self-notes

Three quick fields each day: what got done, what's blocked, and what I'd do differently. Builds into the daily report and helps me reflect honestly without bogging down the workflow.

12

Multiple report formats

Different audiences get different views: Full Report (everything), Project Summary (one-pager for the office), Daily Report (end-of-day update), Weekly Digest (trip wrap-up), and the Master Log — a spreadsheet of every drive, workday, and receipt across every job, ever.

Behind The Scenes How the data flows

📡

Offline-first

Works at any job site, with or without cell service. Everything saves locally first; the cloud backs up the moment service returns.

🤖

AI vision

Anthropic Claude reads odometer photos for mileage, extracts vendor / amount / category from receipts, and writes descriptions for site photos automatically. Uploaded videos get a still-frame thumbnail extracted on the fly so reports show clean clickable previews instead of giant inline players.

📍

GPS-stamped events

Every event — every photo, segment start/stop, and receipt — carries a timestamp and GPS coordinates captured at the moment it happened.

☁️

Cloud-synced

Phone and laptop stay in lockstep. Capture a photo in the parking lot at Chick-fil-A, open the laptop at the hotel — the photo is already there.

"When the office asks about a job — when I arrived, what was done, who I spoke with, what was spent — the answer is one search away, already organized."

What The Office Gets The two deliveries on the receiving end

📧 End-of-Day Update Email

When today has logged activity, a summary email is generated and sent. One tap from the app, or auto-prompted after 4pm.

  • Time on site today
  • Drive time + miles driven
  • Photos captured
  • Receipts + expense total
  • What got done, blockers, follow-ups

📦 Closeout Package (per job)

When a job is closed, the app auto-generates a complete delivery — a single full report with every section, plus separate PDFs by category if needed.

  • Discrepancies Found (any flagged photos surfaced at the top with reason + proposed solution)
  • Project Summary (one-page overview)
  • Job Photos Report — grouped by system (Equipment / Fans / Switching / Site), then by phase (Before / During / After), all GPS-stamped
  • Job Videos — POV clips as clickable thumbnails, click any to play
  • Travel + Time Report (day-by-day GPS-stamped time log with route links)
  • Expense Report (itemized per job, AI-extracted from receipt photos)
  • Notes + Contacts Report (contact log, comm log, issues flagged)
