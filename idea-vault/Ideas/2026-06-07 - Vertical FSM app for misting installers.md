# Vertical FSM app for misting / outdoor-cooling installers

**Problem** — Solo and 1–5 person trade crews (misting, patio cooling, awning,
shade) still run on paper invoices, generic spreadsheets, and phone scheduling.
The big field-service platforms (ServiceTitan ~18% market share / 100k+
contractors, Housecall Pro) are built for 20+ employee HVAC shops at
$200–500/mo — overkill and overpriced for small crews. That niche is openly
underserved.

**Solution** — A mobile-first field-service app for the misting/outdoor-cooling
niche, at **$29–49/mo**. You've effectively already built the engine: the
MistAmerica Job Tracker (geofenced trip logging, photo/system grouping, S-Pen
as-built markup, GC closeout reports, EOD emails). Productize it.

**Why Lyle** — You are the customer. A decade in the field means you know the
exact workflow, and you already have a working app + relationships with other
installers to pilot it.

**First step** — Strip the MistAmerica-specific bits, add a multi-tenant login,
and DM 10 misting / patio-cooling installers you know to pilot it free.

**Effort / cost** — Moderate (the app exists; the work is multi-tenancy + auth +
billing). Validate with free pilots before building billing.

**Risks / unknowns** — Niche size; whether small crews will pay monthly; support
load as a solo founder.

**Sources / links** — Superframeworks (underserved micro-SaaS niches);
BusinessResearchInsights (HVAC FSM market $1.51B in 2026 → $6.29B by 2035, 17.3%
CAGR). Seeded from the 2026-06 Hermes scout pass.
