📖 Quick Operation Reminder
Two things to remember:
🌀 Each canopy has its own FAN switch. The fan moves cool air. No fan running = no cooling, even if mist is on.
💧 The MIST is separate, controlled at the pump. Main pump switch turns mist on/off for everything. OMD has an extra cutoff that only affects OMD mist.
For cool mist at any canopy, ALL of these must be ON:
1. The main pump switch (controls all mist)
2. The fan switch for that canopy (on the same column as the lights and heaters)
3. For OMD specifically: the OMD cutoff at the pump too
🛠️ Common Issues
No mist anywhere
Main pump switch is likely off. Turn it ON. If it's already on and still no mist — tap the red button above to notify service. Do not touch the RO / filter equipment.
OP canopy mists fine but OMD doesn't
Check the OMD MIST CUTOFF (separate switch at the pump). Make sure it's ON. If on and still no OMD mist — notify service.
Fan running but no mist at that canopy
Confirm the main pump switch is ON. (For OMD, also the OMD cutoff.) If both are on and still no mist, nozzles may be clogged — notify service.
Mist visible but no air movement at that canopy
That canopy's fan switch is OFF. Turn the fan switch on (on the column near the lights and heaters).
Loud knocking or screeching from the pump room
Switch the main pump OFF immediately and tap the red button above. Do not keep running it.
Visible drip, leak, or wet spot
Note where it is and tap the red button above. A photo helps if you can take one and email it later to service@mistamerica.com.
Hard-water spots returning on cars or surfaces
The RO filter cartridge may need replacement. Notify service via the red button above — do not adjust the RO yourself.
⛔ Do Not Touch Equipment

Do not adjust the pump, RO filter, valves, or any of the equipment in the pump room. Everything is preset at install. Touching it makes the problem worse, not better.

If a breaker has tripped, tap the red button above and wait. Service will guide you on the correct breaker reset for your specific setup.
