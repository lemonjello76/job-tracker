# Scout pull — Aging-in-place funnel (info product)

> Pulled from Google Drive (untitled doc, Oct 2025). Older find, captured now.
> A video-to-funnel info-product play targeting adult children of aging parents.

## The idea
Short videos dramatizing elderly home-safety dangers (no grab bars, dark
hallways, loose rugs, low toilet, slippery floors, front steps) → post to FB / YT
/ IG / TikTok → funnel to a GoHighLevel page selling a **$7–27 PDF safety
checklist** ("Is Your Parents' Home Safe? 47-Point Safety Checklist"). Timed to
launch ~3 weeks out to **capture Q4 holiday visits** (people see the risks when
they visit parents).

## Playbook
- **Video:** 15–60s, hook in first 3s ("Oh no, that's my mom/dad"); DIY via
  Canva/CapCut + stock, or film 5–7 scenarios in a day; captions (85% watch
  muted). Target adults 40–65.
- **Funnel (GHL):** problem/solution landing page → free "5-Minute Safety
  Assessment" opt-in → upsell full checklist → email series → upsell to
  consultation / home-assessment service.
- **CTAs to test:** "Comment SAFE for the checklist," "Visiting family this
  holiday? Get our checklist."
- **Calendar:** Wk1 produce 10–15 variations · Wk2 build funnel + test · Wk3
  launch daily.

## Monetization angles raised
- Sell the PDF checklist directly, **and/or**
- **Lead gen:** sell qualified leads to home-modification contractors, or
  affiliate home-safety products (grab bars, lighting, ramps).
