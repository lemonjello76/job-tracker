01The Full Job Lifecycle

Works for any job type — new install, service call, RO setup, warranty work. Different starting point, same capture and closeout flow. Three phases. The app captures everything in each phase automatically, then turns it into deliverables at closeout.

1
Before Arrival

New install: office sends construction set. Service call: ticket + previous install record. Different start, same prep.

  • Upload PDF drawings / work order to Job Files
  • AI extracts site contacts from the document
  • Pick checklist template (new install / service call / RO setup / warranty)
  • Tap START DRIVE → GPS-stamped time + odometer photo + AI-read miles
2
On Site

Geofence auto-detects site arrival. Drift monitor catches anything > 1/2 mi away.

  • Tap START WORKDAY → on-site timer begins
  • Photos auto-described by AI, tagged by System (Equipment / Fans / Switching / Site)
  • Receipts AI-extracted to amount / vendor / category
  • Videos uploaded with auto-extracted thumbnails
  • Flag any discrepancy on a photo with a solution note
  • Sketch as-built lines on the office's PDF drawings (S-Pen / finger)
  • Daily EOD email auto-sent to office at 4pm
3
Closeout

End of install — three buttons, three audiences, three records.

  • 🏗️ GC Completion Report → general contractor
  • 📘 User Guide → operator / customer
  • 📱 QR Help Sticker → onto the equipment
  • Every send auto-BCCs office + Lyle
  • Every action logged to the Comm Log (audit trail)
  • Master Log spreadsheet exports for billing or legal

03Behind The Scenes

Every event captured in real time, cloud-synced the instant it happens, AI-processed where possible. The data is the moat.

📡
Offline-First
Works at any job site, with or without cell service. Everything saves to local IndexedDB first; the cloud catches up the moment service returns.
☁️
Cloud-Synced
Firebase Firestore syncs phone + Surface Pro automatically. Capture a photo in the CFA parking lot, open the laptop at the hotel — already there.
🤖
AI Vision
Claude reads odometer photos for mileage, extracts vendor / amount / category from receipts, auto-describes site photos, pulls contacts from work orders.
📍
GPS-Stamped Events
Every photo, segment start/stop, and receipt carries a timestamp and GPS coordinates captured at the moment it happened. Court-grade provenance.

04This Is What Works.

Every event captured three independent ways. Every deliverable triple-copied to the office. Every send timestamped to the second. This is operational standard now — not aspirational, not optional.

Every job leaves three independent records behind.

Local device. Cloud database. Office inbox. Each captures the same event from a different angle, in different storage, at the same instant. There is no scenario where activity goes missing. There is no scenario where the office can claim they didn't know. There is no scenario where the customer can say it wasn't documented. The audit trail is the floor — not the ceiling.

Local
IndexedDB on the phone — works offline, GPS-stamped the moment of capture. Survives no signal, dead Wi-Fi, dropped calls.
Cloud
Firebase Firestore — replicated to laptop within seconds. Queryable, exportable, time-indexed forever.
Office
Every deliverable BCC's service@mistamerica.com and robertl@mistamerica.com. The office and the direct supervisor receive a copy automatically. No exceptions.

1

☁️ Cloud Sync Status

Live indicator. Pulses on every write. Tap to force-sync. Phone + laptop see the same data within seconds — anywhere, any time.

2

🛣 Trip Selector + Totals

Multi-job road trips group automatically. One tap produces a trip-wide totals report covering every job done that week — hours, miles, receipts, day-by-day breakdown.

3

📧 EOD Email Button + 4 PM Banner

Auto-summary of the day's activity (hours, miles, photos, expenses, notes) emailed to office. After 4 PM, a banner reminds if today has unsent activity.

4

📍 Pin Site Location

Tap "Pin Spot" on arrival to drop a GPS anchor. From then on, the app auto-tags any time within 1/2 mile as "on-site." Drift alert if you wander > 1/2 mile while clocked in.

5

📊 Live Dashboard

Drive hours, on-site hours, miles, and expenses update in real time as the day unfolds. No spreadsheet tally at the end — the numbers are always current.

6

🚗 Start Drive / 🏗️ Start Workday / ⛽ Quick Gas

GPS-stamped time segments. Odometer photos auto-read by AI. Gas receipts AI-extracted to amount / vendor / category. One-tap captures.

7

📷 Capture Grid (10 one-tap inputs)

Photos (AI-described), Videos (auto-thumbnailed), Receipts (AI-extracted), Files (PDFs markable with S-Pen), Notes, Contacts, Checklist, Comm Log, Discrepancies, Manual Hours. Everything tagged with GPS + timestamp at the moment of capture.

8

📘 Fillable Closeout Sections

User Guide details: OP / OMD switches, pump location, OMD cutoff, RO drain. GC Closeout: scope, as-built notes (deviations from spec), what's NOT in scope, GC sign-off. Fill once, generates two complete reports.

9

🏗️ + 📘 + 📱 Three Closeout Deliverables

Three taps at job's end produce three distinct documents for three different audiences: GC Completion Report → general contractor (BCC office + supervisor), User Guide → operator/customer, QR Help Sticker → printable, sticks on equipment, scans alert office in real time.

10

📋 Master Log + 🔍 Photo Search

Master Log: spreadsheet of every drive, workday, and receipt across every job ever, with heat map, route links, GPS verification, CSV + PDF export — legal-grade documentation. Photo Search: find any photo across every job by tag, area, or notes.
