# Scout pull — TradeOS contractor platform (user journey)

> Pulled from Google Drive (`Contractor Platform User Journey`). This is the
> **productized version of the vertical-FSM idea** — the misting-installer app
> concept written up as a full product walkthrough.

## What it is
An all-in-one platform for traveling contractors, told through "Mike" (45, HVAC/
misting, travels 200+ days/yr, 3 employees). Working name **TradeOS**.

## Modules
- **AI Triage (active):** customers scan a QR code on the equipment → branded
  troubleshooting flow (won't turn on / low pressure / leaking) → deflects after-
  hours calls, auto-routes work orders. Directly solves Mike losing $2–5k/mo on
  emergency calls + 2–3 hrs/day on the phone.
- **Lead Generator (upgrade):** automated lead gen from permits.
- **Travel App (upgrade):** route/travel optimization, hotels with trailer
  parking.
- **Safety App (upgrade):** emergency alerts / "nobody knows where I am" — solo
  traveler safety.

## Onboarding
Sign up → company profile → import customers (CSV from QuickBooks/Excel) → pick
equipment types (pre-built troubleshooting logic) → customize flows + escalation
→ generate QR codes (download PDF or order printed stickers) → set service-manager
contact + routing.
